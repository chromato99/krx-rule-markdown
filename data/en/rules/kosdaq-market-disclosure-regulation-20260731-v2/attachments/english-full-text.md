CHAPTER  I.  GENERAL  PROVISIONS

§1. Purpose

The  purpose  of  this  Part  is  to  stipulate  the  matters  necessary  for  reporting,  disclosing  and
managing  the  corporate  information,  etc.  of  KOSDAQ-listed  corporations  in  the  KOSDAQ
market established by the Korea Exchange (hereinafter referred to as “the Exchange”) pursuant
to  Article  391  of  the  Financial  Investment  Services  and  Capital  Markets  Act  (hereinafter
referred to as "the Act"). (Amended on January 28, 2009; July 22, 2015)

§3. Applicable Standards

(1)  The  financial  matters  applied  in  this  Part  shall  be  based  on  the  financial  statements
(referring to the consolidated financial statements, in the case of a KOSDAQ-listed corporation
that is a holding company or a parent company; the same hereinafter) revised according to the
result of the audit by an auditor prescribed in Subparagraph 7 of Article 2 of the Act on External
Audit (hereinafter referred to as "CPA auditor"). Provided that, in cases where a listed foreign
corporation prepared its financial statements in accordance with the international accounting
standards  established  by  the  International  Accounting  Standards  Board  or  the  generally
accepted  accounting  principles  in  the  United  States  of America  (“US  GAAP”)  and  had  it
audited according to the laws of the home country or the country where the company’s stocks
are  listed,  it  may  be  based  on  the  concerned  financial  statements  and  the  accounting
standards/principles  adapted  for  the  preparation  of  the  concerned  financial  statements.
(Amended on January 28, 2009; February 22, 2013; May 2, 2019)

§4. Faithful Fulfillment of Disclosure Obligations and Responsibility Thereof

(1) Each KOSDAQ-listed corporation shall disclose all the matters to be disclosed pursuant to
the provisions of this Part and other corporate matters that may have influence on its stock
price, in a voluntary, faithful and timely manner. (Amended on January 28, 2009; December
28, 2016)

(2) Each KOSDAQ-listed corporation shall ensure that the details of corporate information are
not leaked or disseminated in the KOSDAQ Market in the form of rumors or news, etc. before

such information is publicly disclosed pursuant to the provisions of paragraph (1). (Amended
on January 28, 2009)

(3) For the matters arising from the breach of the provisions of paragraphs (1) and (2) by a
KOSDAQ-listed  corporation,  the  concerned  corporation  shall  bear  the  responsibility.
(Amended on January 28, 2009)

Enforcement Rules due to an unavoidable reason, it shall be deemed that the report has been
made on the day on which the cause for reporting occurred; the same hereinafter in the proviso
to [§6-2(1)], the proviso to [§7(1)] and [§7(2)] (limited to cases where reporting is required on
the day on which the cause for reporting arises), [§12(4)] and [§30]) on which the cause for
reporting  has  occurred.  Provided  that,  in  cases  where,  among  subparagraphs  1(c),  2(a)(ix),
2(b)(vii) and 2(c)(iv), the debtor falls under any of subparagraphs 3(b)(i) through (iii) and in

10% (5% for a large-sized corporation) or more of the sales amount of the latest fiscal
year has been made; and

(e) Case where a factory producing 10% or more of the sales amount of the latest fiscal
year has suspended its production or has been shut down.

KOSDAQ-listed corporation: [October 1, 2025]

(i) When the occurrence of a serious accident is reported to the Minister of Employment

(a) Case where there has been any of the following events or decisions about the securities
issued by the concerned KOSDAQ-listed corporation:

(i) When a decision on capital increase or reduction has been made;

3. Foreign institutional investors who are engaged in the same or similar businesses as the
businesses  of  the  entities  under  subparagraphs  1  and  2,  and  their  executive  officers  and
employees;

4. Broadcasting business operators under the Broadcasting Act and news agencies such as
newspapers  and  communications  under  the  Act  on  the  Promotion  of  Newspapers,  Etc.

§16. Indemnification for Predictions

In cases where, with respect to the fair disclosure obligations regarding the future predictions
or projections, a KOSDAQ-listed corporation makes  fair disclosure in  any of the following

4. With regard to the relevant statement or indication, a clear warning note shall have been
added saying that the predictions may differ from the actual performances.

PART  II.  BUSINESS  DEVELOPMENT  COLLECTIVE  INVESTMENT  VEHICLE

CHAPTER  I.  GENERAL  PROVISIONS

§50-2 Purpose

The  purpose  of  this  Part  is  to  stipulate  the  matters  necessary  for  reporting,  disclosing  and
managing  the  corporate  information,  etc.  of  corporations  listed  on  collective  investment
securities of a business development collective investment vehicle pursuant to Articles 229-2
and 391 of the Act.

[March 4, 2026]

§50-3. Definitions

(1) The definitions of terms used in this Part shall be as follows:

1.  The  term  “business  development  collective  investment  business  entity”  refers  to  the
business development collective investment business entity under Article 9, Paragraph
31 of the Act;

2.  The term “business development collective investment vehicle” refers to the business
development collective investment vehicle under Article 229, Subparagraph 6 of the
Act;

3.  The  term  “business  development  collective  investment  securities”  refers  to  the

securities issued by a business development collective investment vehicle;

4.  The term “business development investment trust” refers to the business development

collective investment vehicle established as an investment trust;

5.  The  term  “business  development  investment  company”  refers  to  the  business
development collective investment vehicle established as an investment company;

6.  The  term  “listed  company  that  issued  business  development  investment  trust
beneficiary  certificates”  refers  to  the  business  development  collective  investment
business entity that issued beneficiary certificates listed on the KOSDAQ Market;

7. The term “listed company that issued business development investment company stocks”
refers to the business development investment company that issued stocks listed on the
KOSDAQ Market;

8.  The  term  “listed  company  that  issued  business  development  collective  investment
securities” refers to the business development collective investment business entity and
business development investment company that issued business development collective
investment securities listed on the KOSDAQ Market;

9. The term “primary investment target company” refers to the primary investment target

company under Article 81, Paragraph 4, Subparagraph 1 of the Act;

10.  The  term  “disclosure  officer”  refers  to  a  corporate  director  in  the  case  of  a  listed
company that issued business development investment company stocks, and in the case
of  a  listed  company  that  issued  business  development  investment  trust  beneficiary
certificates, to a person designated as a disclosure officer among the employees of the
business development collective investment business entity; and

11. The term “unfaithful disclosure” refers to a case where a listed company that issued

business  development  collective  investment  securities  falls  under  any  of  the  cases  of
non-compliance  with  disclosure  obligations,  reversal  or  disclosure  or  change  of
disclosure, by failing to faithfully fulfill the reporting obligations under the provisions
of this Part or reversing or changing the details of the matters already reported.

(2) In addition, the definitions of terms used in the Part shall be in accordance with the Act, the
Enforcement Decree of the Act, the Enforcement Rules of the Act, and other relevant statutes,
and the rules and regulations established by the Exchange.

[March 4, 2026]

§50-4. Applicable Standard

(1) In this Part, “total assets” shall be applied based on the total assets at end of the immediately
preceding quarter. Provided that, in cases of initial listings listing after merger, or relisting after
a spin-off merger after the end of the immediately preceding quarter, it shall be applied based
on the total assets at the time of listing for each respective reason.

(2) In this Part, “valuation amount” shall be applied based on the valuation results pursuant to
Article  229-2,  Paragraph  3  of  the  Act  and  Article  241-3,  Paragraph  7  of  the  Enforcement
Decree of the Act.

[March 4, 2026]

§50-5. Faithful Fulfillment of Disclosure Obligations, etc.

(1) A listed company that issued business development collective investment securities shall
faithfully report matters subject to disclosure obligation under this Part.

(2) A listed company that issued business development collective investment securities shall
ensure that the details of corporate information are not leaked or disseminated in the securities
market  in  the  form  of  rumors  or  news,  etc.  before  such  information  is  publicly  disclosed
pursuant to paragraph (1).

[March 4, 2026]

§50-6. Methods for Reporting and Document Submission

(1) The matters to be disclosed and reported to the Exchange by a listed company that issued
business development collective investment securities pursuant to the provisions of this shall

be prepared and submitted electronically as prescribe in Article 436 of the Act, and in the case
of electronic submission, the submission in writing (including submission via facsimile) may
be waived. In this case, Part III of these regulations shall be applied.

(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph (1), when the Exchange deems it inevitable, a
listed company that issued business development collective investment securities may report
by filing of written submission (including submission via facsimile).

§50-13. Types of Unfaithful Disclosure

In  the  case  where  a  listed  company  that  issued  business  development  collective  investment

(a) Case of failing to make disclosure of the matters to be disclosed pursuant to [§50-7]

through [§50-12] and [§50-22] by the relevant reporting deadlines; or

(b) Case of making false or incorrect disclosure of the matters to be disclosed pursuant
to  [§50-7]  through  [§50-12]  and  [§50-22],  or  making  a  disclosure  without  stating
material matters.

2. Reversals of Disclosures: Case of making a disclosure that completely cancels or denies
the details already disclosed pursuant to [§50-7] through [§50-12] or other disclosure with
a content equivalent thereto; or

3. Making changes to existing disclosure: Cases where material matters of content already
disclosed pursuant to [§50-7] and [§50-10] are changed and the details prescribed in the
Enforcement Rules are disclosed.

[March 4, 2026]

listed company that issued business development collective investment securities shall comply
without delay unless there are special circumstances.

[March 4, 2026]

(1) Notwithstanding the provisions of [§50-13], the Exchange shall not regard it as an unfaithful

1. When it is unavoidable under other statutes, regulations, etc.;

2. When a natural disaster, sudden and significant change in the economic situations or other
incidents equivalent thereto have occurred;

3. Case where it is deemed necessary for the public interests and protection of investors; and

4. In addition, cases where the concerned KOSDAQ-listed corporation proves that it is not
culpable for the cause, or the Exchange deems it to be a minor matter that has no or little
influence on the stock price.

(2)  In  applying  the  provisions  of  paragraph  (1),  the  Exchange  may  request  the  relevant

PART  III.  REPORTTING,  ETC.  BY  ELECTRONIC  DOCUMENTS
BY KOSDAQ-LISTED CORPORATIONS, ETC.
(Amended on January 28, 2009; March 4, 2026)

CHAPTER  I.  GENERAL  PROVISIONS

§51. Objectives

The purpose of this Part is to stipulate the matters necessary with respect to filing of a statement,
a report, or other document or material (hereinafter referred to as the “reports, etc.”) with the
Exchange by KOSDAQ-listed corporations, etc. by an electronic document,  pursuant  to  the
provisions of Article 385(3) of the Enforcement Decree of the Act. (Amended on January 28,
2009)

§52. Definitions

(1) The term “submitter” in this Part shall mean a person who is obliged to submit electronic
documents under [§53]. (Amended on January 28, 2009)

(2) The term “digital signature” in this Part shall mean a piece of information that is generated
by using the digital signature creating key through the asymmetric cryptogram and unique to
an electronic document, which enables to verify the identity of the person who submitted the
electronic document and whether or not the electronic document has been modified. (Amended
on January 28, 2009)

(3) The term “asymmetric cryptogram” in this Part shall mean a cryptographic method in which
the  key  used  for  encryption  of  information  and  the  key  used  for  restoration  of  encrypted
information are different from each other.

(4) The term “digital signature creating key” in this Part shall mean a piece of information in
digital form utilized for digital signature.

(5) In addition to paragraphs (1) through (4), the terms used in this Part shall have the same
meanings given to them in the Act, the Enforcement Decree of the Act, the Enforcement Rule
of the Act and other relevant laws and the rules and regulations of the Exchange.

§53. Reports, etc. filed in Electronic Documents

The reports, etc. that are filed by an electronic document under this Part shall be each of the

followings: (Amended on January 28, 2009; February 22, 2013; July 22, 2015)

1.  The  reports,  etc.  that  are  submitted  to  the  Exchange  pursuant  to  this  Regulation,  the
KOSDAQ Market Listing Regulation and the KOSDAQ Market Business Regulation, etc.;

2. [Deleted on January 28, 2009]

3. The disclosure documents under [§68]; and

4. Other documents, etc. that the Exchange deems necessary to be submitted in a form of
electronic document.

§54. Operation of Electronic Disclosure System

(1) The Exchange shall operate an electronic disclosure system that is capable of receiving the
reports,  etc.  submitted  in  a  form  of  electronic  document  pursuant  to  [§53].  (Amended  on
January 28, 2009)

(2) The Exchange shall make available the reports, etc. that are received in a form of electronic
document (including the reports, etc. received in an electronic form pursuant to [§5(1)]) for the
public use through information and communication networks. Provided that this provision shall
not apply to the case where the Exchange deems it necessary.

CHAPTER  II.  PREPERATION  AND  SUBMISSION  OF  ELECTRONIC  DOCUM
ENTS

§55. Registration of Submitters of Electronic Documents

(1) Each submitter of electronic documents shall register his/her personal information with the
Exchange in advance in a manner specified in the Enforcement Rules and shall do the same
when the registered information is changed.

(2) When a submitter has been registered or re-registered the changes in registered information
with  the  Exchange  pursuant  to  paragraph  (1),  the  Exchange  shall  supply  the  necessary
information, including the unique identification number to the concerned submitter. (Amended
on January 28, 2009)

§56. Submission, etc. of Electronic Documents

(1)  A  submitter  shall  include  the  matters  necessary  for  identifying  the  submitter  and  the
information necessary for receiving, classifying and processing the electronic documents in the
reports, etc. submitted in a form of electronic document.

(2)  When  submitting  the  reports,  etc.  electronically  in  accordance  with  paragraph  (1),  the
submitter shall attach the source documents.

(3)  Submitters  may  submit  hard  copies  of  an  attachment  which  is  deemed  difficult  to  be
included in or attached to the electronic documents.

§57. Receipt of Electronic Documents and Notification Thereof

(1) The Exchange may refuse to receive the reports, etc. submitted electronically by a submitter,
if the reports, etc. are not prepared and submitted in accordance with the provisions of this Part.

(2) The  Exchange  shall  notify  the  submitter  of  the  receipt  of  the  reports,  etc.  submitted  by
means of electronic document by using the information and communication networks.

§58. Submission of Corrected Documents

In cases where a submitter received a request for correction of a submitted electronic document
or  intends  to  correct  it  voluntarily,  the  submitter  shall  submit  the  corrected  version  of  the
electronic document, together with the details of the corrections.

§59. Documents prepared by Persons Other Than the Submitter

From among the documents to be attached to the reports, etc. pursuant to [§53], those that are
prepared by a person other than the submitter, including a certified public accountant, shall
digitally be signed by the person who prepared them before they are given to the submitter.
(Amended on January 28, 2009)

§60. Number of Copies of Electronic Document to be submitted

In cases where a submitter has submitted the reports, etc. in a form of electronic document, it
shall be deemed to have met the number of copies to be submitted as stipulated in the relevant
Regulations of the Exchange.

§61. Validity, etc. of Digital Signature

(1) When intending to submit the reports, etc. in a form of electronic document, a submitter
shall affix a digital signature on them.

(2)  In  cases  where  a  submitter  has  affixed  a  digital  signature  on  an  electronic  document
pursuant to paragraph (1), it shall be deemed that the submitter has signed or affixed his/her
seal on the document.

(3)  The  registration  and  certification  of  the  digital  signature  for  signing  the  electronic
documents digitally pursuant to paragraph (1) and other matters relating to the digital signature

shall be accomplished in accordance with the manner stipulated in the Regulation on Securities
Issuance and Disclosure. (Amended on January 28, 2009)

§62. Failure of Electronic Disclosure System

(1) In cases where, due to a failure of the electronic disclosure system, the submission of the
electronic documents under the provisions of this Part is not possible, the submitter shall submit
the reports, etc. under [§53] to the Exchange in writing or via facsimile, etc. and In such cases,
the electronic document files shall be attached. (Amended on January 28, 2009)

(2) When the electronic disclosure system is restored, the Exchange shall immediately register
the electronic document files submitted under the provisions of paragraph (1) in the electronic
disclosure system.

§64. Definitions

(1) The  term  "collective  investment  business  entities"  in  this  Part  shall  mean  the  collective
investment business entities under Article 8(4) of the Act. (Amended on January 28, 2009)

(2) The term "investment company, etc." in this Part shall mean the investment company, etc.
under Article 182(1) of the Act. (Amended on January 28, 2009)

(3) The  term  "disclosure  officer"  in  this  Part  shall  mean  a  full-time  director  (in  the  case  of
investment company, etc., it refers to a corporate director) or a person falling under Article 401-
2(1)3  of  the  Commercial  Act,  who  is  in  charge  of  corporate  disclosure  representing  the
concerned collective investment business entity, etc. Provided that, in the case of a company

that has filed an application for commencement of rehabilitation procedures or has received the
court  ruling  on  commencement  of  the  rehabilitation  procedures  pursuant  to  the  Debtor
Rehabilitation  and  Bankruptcy Act,  the  custodian  under Article  74  of  the  same Act  or  the
protective custodian under Article 85 of the same Act shall be deemed as the disclosure officer.
(Amended on September 12, 2008; January 28, 2009)

(4) The  term  "unfaithful  disclosure"  in  this  Part shall  refer  to  the  case  where  the  collective
investment business entities, etc. fail to faithfully fulfill the disclosure obligations under the
relevant statutes and this Part. (Amended on January 28, 2009)

(5) The term “corporations required to disclose their voting rights” in this Part shall mean the
KOSDAQ-listed corporations, from among the corporations required to disclose their voting
rights under the provisions of Article 87(7) of the Act. (Amended on January 28, 2009)

§65. Faithful Fulfillment of Disclosure Obligations, etc.

(1) The collective investment business entities, etc. shall faithfully and voluntarily disclose the
matters to be disclosed pursuant to the relevant statutes and this Part. (Amended on January 28,
2009)

(2) The collective investment business entities, etc. shall, before disclosing the matters to be
disclosed under paragraph (1), neither leak nor spread the details of the concerned information
in the KOSDAQ Market in the form of rumors, news, etc. (Amended on January 28, 2009)

§66. Methods of Reporting and Submission of Documents

The matters that the collective investment business entities, etc. are obliged to disclose to the
Exchange pursuant to this Part shall be submitted in a form of electronic document pursuant to
Part  II.  Provided  that,  in  cases  where  the  Exchange  deems  it  unavoidable,  they  may  be
submitted via postal service, facsimile or in person. (Amended on January 28, 2009)

§67. Designation of Disclosure Officer

(1) The collective investment business entities, etc. shall designate disclosure officers (one each
for  the  principal  and  the  associate)  who  will  be  responsible  for  the  duty  of  disclosure
representing the relevant company. In such cases, the investment company, etc. may, aside from
the corporate director of the concerned company, designate a person-in-charge of the company,
which  is  deemed  proper,  from  among  the  collective  investment  business  entity,  the  trust
business  entity,  the  investment  trader,  the  investment  broker  and  the  fund  accounting  and
administration company, with which it has entered into a business entrustment contract, as the
associate disclosure officer. (Amended on January 28, 2009)

(2) The collective investment business entities, etc. shall, when designating or changing their
disclosure officers pursuant to paragraph (1), notify the Exchange of the fact without delay,
together with a document stating that the concerned disclosure officer will faithfully fulfill the

disclosure obligations. (Amended on January 28, 2009)
