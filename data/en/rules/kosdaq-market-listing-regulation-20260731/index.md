PART I. GENERAL PROVISIONS

CHAPTER I. GENERAL PROVISIONS

§1. Purpose

The purpose of this Regulation is to prescribe the matters necessary for the Korea Exchange to
examine  the  securities  to  be  listed  on  the  KOSDAQ  Market  and  manage  corporations  and
securities  listed  on  the  KOSDAQ  Market  in  accordance  with Article  390  of  the  Financial
Investment Services and Capital Markets Act.

§2. Definitions

(1) The terms used in this Regulation shall be defined as follows: [March 16, 2022] (Amended
on December 27, 2023)

1.  The term “listing” means the granting of qualifications to be traded on the KOSDAQ
Market for securities of a specific issue, except as otherwise provided in this Regulation.
In such cases, the types of listing shall be classified into each of the followings:

(a)   Initial  listing:  Case  of  securities  of  an  issue  that  have  not  been  listed  on  the
KOSDAQ Market are listed for the first time. Provided that, the case of listing after
merger  of  a  special  purpose  acquisition  company  pursuant  to  (f)(ii)  shall  be
excluded;

(b) Backdoor listing: Cases where, as stipulated in [§33] or [§64], there is an effect of
listing equity securities of a corporation, which is not listed on the KOSDAQ or
KOSPI  market,  with  respect  to  a  merger,  all-inclusive  stock  swap,  takeover  of
business, acquisition of assets, investment in kind, etc.;

(c)  Re-listing:  Cases  where,  as  stipulated  in  [§39],  common  stocks  issued  by  a
corporation that is established through spin-off, merger after spin-off, or merger of
a KOSDAQ Market-listed corporation are listed on the KOSDAQ Market;

6

(d) Listing change: Case of listing after changing the class, name of issue, par value,
quantity,  etc.  of  a  listed  security.  Provided  that,  the  listing  that  falls  under
supplementary listing shall be excluded;

(e) Supplementary listing: Case of listing new securities issued by a corporation listed
on the KOSDAQ Market, which is identical to the securities that are already listed;

(f) Listing after merger: Case of listing stocks subsequent to a merger between a special
purpose acquisition company and a corporation that is not listed on the KOSDAQ
Market. In such cases, the types of the listing after merger  shall be classified as
follows:

(i)  Listing of new stocks issued by the special purpose acquisition company in the
case where such special purpose acquisition company continues to operate after
the merger; and

(ii)  Listing  of stocks issued by  the corporation  that is  not  listed on the  KOSDAQ
Market in the case where such corporation continues to operate after the merger.

2.  The term  “listing applicant”  refers to a person who applies for  a preliminary listing
review or listing examination in order to list securities on the KOSDAQ Market. In
such cases, the types of listing applicant shall be classified as follows:

(a) Applicant for initial listing:

(b) Applicant for backdoor listing;

(c) Applicant for re-listing;

(d) Applicant for listing change;

(e) Applicant for supplementary listing; and

(f) Applicant for listing after merger.

3.  The term “KOSDAQ-listed corporation” refers to a corporation of which securities are

listed on the KOSDAQ Market.

4.  The term “common-stock-listing corporation” refers to a corporation of which common

stocks are listed on the KOSDAQ Market.

5.  The  term  “KOSPI-listed  corporation”  refers  to  a  corporation  of  which  stocks  or

depository receipts (DRs) of foreign stocks are listed on the KOSPI Market.

6.  The term “KONEX-listed corporation” refers to a corporation of which stocks are listed

on the KONEX Market.

the KOSDAQ Market, KOSPI Market or KONEX Market.

8.  The term “backdoor listing target company” refers to an unlisted corporation (including
a corporation that issues equity securities other than stocks) or a KONEX Market-listed
corporation that is subject to backdoor listing.

9.  The term  “listed issue”  means a stock, DRs  of foreign  stocks, subscription  warrant,
subscription  right  certificate,  and  beneficiary  certificates  which  are  issued  by  a
KOSDAQ-listed corporation and listed on the KOSDAQ Market in accordance with
this Regulation. (Amended on March 4, 2026)

§3. Applicable Standards for Accounting Standards and Financial Statements

(1)  Unless  otherwise  provided  in  this  Regulation,  the  audit  reports  applicable  to  domestic
corporations shall refer to the audit reports prepared by the auditors prescribed in Subparagraph
7 of Article 2 of the Act on External Audit of Stock Companies, etc. (hereinafter referred to as
“the  Act  on  External  Audit”)  subsequent  to  auditing  the  financial  statements  prepared  in
accordance with the Korean International Financial Reporting Standards (hereinafter referred
to as the “K-IFRS”)] under Article 5(1)1 of the Act on External Audit. In such cases, in the case
of  a  corporation  with  a  subsidiary  under  the Act  on  External Audit,  an  audit  report  on  its
consolidated financial statements shall be included.

(2)  Unless  otherwise  provided  in  this  Regulation,  the  audit  reports  applicable  to  foreign
corporations shall refer to the audit reports prepared by the auditors prescribed in Subparagraph
7 of Article 2 of the Act on External Audit or the foreign accounting auditor specified in Article
169(3) of the Act subsequent to auditing the financial statements prepared in accordance with
the accounting standards under any of the following subparagraphs. In such cases, in the case
of a corporation with a subsidiary shall include an audit report on its consolidated financial
statements.

1. K-IFRS;

2. International accounting standards established by the International Accounting Standards
Board (hereinafter referred to as the “international accounting standards”); and

3.  Accounting  standards  that  is  generally  accepted  in  the  United  States  of  America
(hereinafter referred to as the “US GAAP”).

(3) Notwithstanding paragraph (2), in cases where a foreign subsidiary of a listing applicant
has  adopted  the  international  accounting  standards  other  than  those  specified  in  each
subparagraph of paragraph (2) pursuant to the laws and regulations, etc. of its home country,

15

and submitted the documents set forth in the Enforcement Rules, it shall be construed that the
accounting standards in any subparagraph of paragraph (2) have been complied with.

(4)  No  KOSDAQ-listed  foreign  company  shall  change  the  accounting  standards  adopted
[referring  to  accounting  standards  under  any  subparagraph  of  paragraph  (2)]  at  the  time  of
applying for the preliminary listing review to other accounting standards after listing. Provided
that,  as  an  unavoidable  case  such  as  a  change  in  the  financial  investment  laws  of  foreign
countries  pursuant  to Article  169(3)  of  the Act  (hereinafter  referred  to  as  “the  foreign  laws
related  to  financial  investment  business”)  occurs  and  the  Exchange  acknowledges  it  as
acceptable,  the  accounting  standards  may  be  changed  to  any  of  the  standards  set  forth  in
paragraph (2).

(5) An auditor (including an accountant in charge) who has an interest in the listing applicant,
such  as  holding  equities  or  equity-related  bonds  or  acquiring  stock  options  of  the  listing
applicant, may not prepare an audit report under paragraphs (1) or (2).

(6) Unless otherwise provided in this Regulation, matters concerning financial details shall be
applied based on the financial statements in which the opinion of the auditor is ‘unqualified’ or

CHAPTER II. GENERAL OPERATION OF LISTING OF SECURITIES

§4. Prohibition on Partial Listings

(1) A listing applicant shall not be allowed to apply for the listing of a part of securities of the
particular issue to be listed.

(2)  Notwithstanding  paragraph  (1),  in  cases  where  a  corporation  that  has  listed  a  part  of
securities  of  the  particular  issue  on  an  overseas  securities  market  intends  to  list  the  same
securities on the KOSDAQ Market, it shall apply for the listing of the securities other than
those listed on the overseas securities market. In such cases, the total number of issued shares
shall be calculated excluding the quantity listed on the overseas securities market.

activities, changes in major assets, etc.;

(including a preliminary investment prospectus) including any correction made to its

§33. Backdoor Listing
