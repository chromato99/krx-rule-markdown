CHAPTER I. GENERAL PROVISIONS

§1. Purpose

The  purpose  of  these  Enforcement  Rules  is  to  stipulate  the  matters  necessary  for  the
enforcement of the Market Oversight Regulation. (Amended on March 18, 2020)

§2. Definitions

The terms used in these Enforcement Rules shall have the same meanings given to them
in  the  Market  Oversight  Regulation  (hereinafter  referred  to  as  “the  Regulation”).
(Amended on March 18, 2020)

CHAPTER II. MAINTENANCE OF MARKET RELIABILITY

§2-2.  Selection  of  Members  subject  to  Request  for  Preventative  Measures,  etc.  and
Method Thereof

(1)  Pursuant  to  [§5(4)]  of  the  Regulation,  the  Market  Oversight  Commission
(hereinafter  referred  to  as  “the  Commission”)  may  request  the  members  who  are
implicated  to  the  accounts  falling  under  the  criteria  that  the  Chairperson  of  the
Commission  (hereinafter  referred  to  as  “the  Chairperson”)  separately  determines  to
provide  guidance  for  reliable  order  during  the  market  hours  and  take  the  preventative
measures. In this case, if the accounts fall under any7 of the following subparagraphs,
the Commission may request preventative measure severer than the prior notification of
the refusal of order receipt. (Amended on March 8, 2012; July 31, 2014; November 23,

2. Case of receiving the request for preventative measures by participating in an issue

3. Case of receiving the request for preventative measures because it is judged that
there is a high possibility of falling under an unfair trading in view of such factors as
the repetition of unsound trading pattern, past unsound trading history of the account,
and excessive stock price volatility.

preventative measures  pursuant  to  paragraph (1) shall take  the measures necessary for

refusing to accept the order entrustment as specified by the Commission.: (Amended on

§2-3. Informing of Investment Precaution

Pursuant to [§5(4)] of the Regulation, in the case of falling under any of the following
subparagraphs, the Commission may inform the investors of the matters on investment
precaution  through  such  methods  as  posting  it  on  its  internet  website,  etc.  in  order  to
prevent any damage to investors:

1.  Case  where,  due  to  any  activity  undermining  the  market  order  such  as  unfair
trading, the damage to many inventors has occurred or is expected to occur; and

2. In addition, case where any event having a major impact on the capital market has
taken place or is expected to take place.

[August 27, 2012]

§2-4. Consultation for Members

(1)  Pursuant  to  [§5(4)]  of  the  Regulation,  the  Commission  may  provide  consultation
services  (hereinafter  referred  to  as  the  “consultation”)  for  the  members  in  order  to
support  the  prevention  of  unfair  trading  and  compliance  with  regulations  related  to
business (in the case of trading participants, it refers to the Regulation and the business
standards  of  the  relevant  multilateral  trading  companies;  hereinafter  referred  to  as
“business-related  regulations)  of  Korea  Exchange  (hereinafter  referred  to  as  “the
Exchange”) by the members. (Amended on February 27, 2025)

(2) The Commission may have prior discussions with the members on the target, period,
method, scope of data collection, etc. with regard to the consultation.

(3)  If  deemed  necessary,  the  Commission  may  request  the  members  to  submit  data,
materials, etc. for the consultation.

(4)  When  requesting  members  to  submit  data,  etc.  pursuant  to  paragraph  (3),  the
Commission shall notify the reasons, types of data, submission deadline, etc. in writing.

[April 18, 2016]

§2-5. Procedures and Methods of Consultation for Members

(1)  The  consultation  may  be  conducted  in  the  cases  that  correspond  to  any  of  the
following subparagraphs: (Amended on February 10, 2017; November 23, 2022)

1. When it is specified in the annual consultation plan set by the Commission;

2. When it was requested by a member; or

3. When the Chairperson deems it necessary for the maintenance of market order and
the protection of investors.

(2)  When  requesting  the  consultation  pursuant  to  paragraph  (1)2,  a  member  shall
complete the written request for consultation in accordance with [Annex: Form 17] and
submit it to the Exchange. In such cases, the Commission may reject the request if there
is an investigation or inspection planned or being conducted, of which contents are the
same as or similar to those of the consultation requested by the member.

(3)  The  consultation  shall  be  carried  out  using  such  methods  as  on-site  inspection  by
surveillance staff (limited to staff members in charge of inspection), in writing, or other
means. In such cases, the Commission shall notify the member in advance in writing.

(4)  Notwithstanding  paragraph  (3),  in  cases  where  it  is  deemed  necessary  for  the
efficiency,  etc.  of  consultation,  the  Commission  may  implement  the  consultation  at  a
different venue, such as the Commission, with the consent of the member.

(5)  The  necessary  matters  on  the  detailed  procedures  and  methods  of  the  consultation
shall be determined by the Chairperson.

[April 18, 2016]

§2-6.  Notification  of  Consultation  Results,  etc.

(1)  When  the  consultation  is  completed,  the  Exchange  shall  notify  the  member  of  the
report  on  consultation  results  which  contains  the  matters  necessary  to  support  the
prevention  of  unfair  trading  and  compliance  with  business-related  regulations  by  the
member.

(2)  In  the  case  of  falling  under  any  of  the  following  subparagraphs,  the  Commission
may explain the results of the consultation to the officers in charge of the related tasks
of the member:

1. Case where detailed explanation is necessary in order to facilitate the reflection of
the contents of the consultation by the member; or

2. Case where the consultation was conducted at the request of the member.

(3)  In  cases  where  the  results  of  the  consultation  reveal  the  facts  applicable  to  the
criteria for disciplinary actions under [§14(1)4 through 6] or the criteria for disciplinary
measures under [§14(2)1 through 3], the Commission may notify the relevant member
of  the  matters  requiring  correction  that  state  the  details  of  violations,  required
rectifications and the deadline for the notification of the result of corrective actions.

(4) Notwithstanding paragraph (3), if the consultation reveals that a member is found to
correspond  to  any  of  the  following  subparagraphs,  the  Commission  may  conduct
inspections pursuant to [§16] of the Regulation: (Amended on September 7, 2018)

1.  Case  where  the  significance  of  the  cause  of  violation  noted  in  the  criteria  for
disciplinary measures under subparagraphs 1 through 3 of [§14(1)] or the guidelines
for the imposition of member fine in [Annex 1] was found to be ‘High’;

2. Case where the member has not rectified the matters requiring correction until the
deadline for notification of correction results under paragraph (3); and

3. Case where the Commission deems the action to be against investor protection or
market soundness.

[April 18, 2016]

§2-7.  Consultation  Advisory  Committee

(1)  The  Commission  may  establish  a  consultation  advisory  committee  comprised  of
experts, etc., to provide consultation services for the members.

(2)  The  details  for  organizing  and  operating  of  the  consultation  advisory  committee
shall be determined by the Chairperson.

[April 18, 2016]

§2-8.  Training  for  Stock-listed  Corporations

(1) Pursuant to [§5(4)] of the Regulation, the Commission may provide training sessions
for  prevention  of  unfair  trading  (hereinafter  referred  to  as  the  “training”)  targeting
stock-listed corporations in order to support them with the prevention of unfair trading
practices and the compliance with work-related regulations.

(2)  The  Commission  may  have  prior  discussions  with  stock-listed  corporations  on  the
target, subjects, time and methods, etc. with regard to the training.

[May 31, 2016]

§2-9.  Procedures  and  Methods  of  Training  for  Stock-listed  Corporations

(1) The training may be conducted in the cases that correspond to any of the following
subparagraphs:

1. When it is specified in the annual training plan set by the Commission;

2. When it was requested by a stock-listed corporation; or

3.  When  the  Chairperson  deems  it  necessary  for  the  maintenance  of  fair  trading
practices and for the protection of investors.

(2)  When  requesting  for  the  training  pursuant  to  paragraph  (1)2,  a  stock-listed
corporation  shall  submit  a  written  request  for  prevention  training  completed  in
accordance with [Annex: Form 18] to the Exchange.

(3) The training may be conducted by such methods as in-house training determined by
the Exchange, on-site training by surveillance staff, and online training provided by the
Commission, etc.

(4) The necessary matters on the detailed procedures and methods of the training shall
be determined by the Chairperson.

[May 31, 2016]

§2-10. Consultation on Compliance for Stock-listed Corporations

(1)  Pursuant  to  [§5(4)]  of  the  Regulation,  the  Commission  may  provide  consultation
services on compliance (hereinafter referred to as the “compliance consultation”) for the
stock-listed corporations in order to support the prevention of unfair trade practices.

(2) The matters necessary with respect to the provision of the compliance consultation
noted in paragraph (1) shall be determined by the Chairperson.

[February 10, 2017]

§2-11. Operation of Registration System for Executives and Employees of Stock-listed
Corporations

(1)  Pursuant  to  [§5(4)]  of  the  Regulation,  in  order  to  support  listed  corporations’
preventative  actions  against  unfair  trading  practices,  the  Commission  may  operate  a
system (hereinafter referred to  as the “registration system for executives/employees of
listed  corporations”)  that  registers  personal  information  such  as  name,  date  of  birth,
account  number,  etc.  of  executive  officers  and  employees  (including  major
shareholders)  of  a  listed  corporation,  and,  when  such  registered  executive  officers
and/or  employees  trade  the  stocks  of  the  relevant  listed  corporation  (including  its
affiliated companies), notifies such fact to the relevant listed corporation.

(2)  When  a  stock-listed  corporation  wishes  to  use  the  registration  system  for
executives/employees  of  listed  corporations,  it  shall  submit  an  application  for  use  of
services  for  executives/employees  registration  and  trade  notification  completed  in
accordance  with  [Annex:  Form  No.  19]  with  the  consent  of  the  executive  officer  or
employee who is the owner of the relevant personal information.

(3)  The  matters  necessary  with  respect  to  the  operation  of  the  registration  system  for
executives/employees of listed corporations shall be determined by the Chairperson.

[December 11, 2018]

§18. Types and Reasons for Requesting Members to Take Measures, etc.

(1) The types and reasons for requesting measures against members pursuant to [§22(5)]

implementation  of  the  measures  taken  by  the  Commission  pursuant  to  [§22]  of  the
Regulation.

a. Assessment of the severity of the causes of violations

(i) Factors in assessing the severity of the causes

(a) Whether the executive officers and department heads are involved and the

degree of their involvement, and the number of employees involved;

(b) The extent of awareness of violations;

(c) Whether there is a violation through the member's proprietary account;

(d)  The  appropriateness  of  the  internal  control  system  to  prevent  violations
including measures by members subsequent to their monitoring activities; and

(e) Whether the violation is repeated, etc.

3. Aggravation and mitigation

a. The aggravation or mitigation of a member fine pursuant to [§17, subparagraph 2]
shall be made by raising or lowering the applicable range by one level. Provided that,
if there is a voluntary report noted in [§16(3)], the range may be adjusted upward or
downward by one level.

b. The applicable disciplinary measures in cases where the upper limit of the fine range
is subject to aggravation or the lower limit is subject to mitigation shall be as follows:

(i)

In  the  case  of  aggravation:  Disciplinary  action  under  [§22(1)1-3]  of  the

Regulation; and

(ii)

In the case of mitigation: Disciplinary action under [§22(1)3] of the Regulation.

c. Increase or reduction of the member fine amount

The  member  fine  amount  may  be  increased  or  reduced  within  the  fine  range

considering following factors:

(i)  Manners  or  attitudes  of  the  relevant  member  (the  acceptance  or  denial  of
responsibility, attempts to conceal the violation, etc.);

(ii) Whether a member voluntarily implements corrective or disciplinary measures,
preventive measures, and internal censorship or control processes; and

(iii) Willingness to cooperate in the investigation and inspection, compliance with
the  request  for  documents  or  attendance,  the  extent  of  delay  caused  in  the
investigation or inspection.

4. Reduction of or exemption from member fine, etc.

a.  If a member, who has already been sanctioned in the form of a penalty surcharge
from  the  Financial  Services  Commission  for  the  same  violation,  reports  the  fact
that the member has been sanctioned by the Financial Services Commission within
5  days  from  the  date  of  receiving  the  prior  notice  pursuant  to  [§26]  of  the
Regulation, the member fine based on this standard may be reduced or exempted in
consideration of the amount of penalty surcharge.

b.  If a member, who paid a member fine in accordance with the measure of member
fine  imposition  by  the  Commission,  report  to  the  Commission  the  fact  that  the
member  has  been  sanctioned  against  the  same  violation  in  the  form  of  a  penalty
surcharge by the Financial Services Commission within 30 days from the date of
payment of the penalty surcharge, part or all of the member fine may be refunded
to the member in consideration of the amount of penalty surcharge.

c.  If  a  member  fine  is  imposed  for  each  of  the  multiple  violations  of  a  member
pursuant  to  [§15-2(2)],  the  total  member  fine  shall  not  exceed  the  maximum
amount noted in [§22(1)2] of the Regulation

5. Refund of mistaken payment of member fines

a.  In cases where the member on whom a fine has been imposed under [§22(1)2] of
the  Regulation  requests  a  refund  of  a  mistakenly  paid  fine  by  citing  a  formal
objection  pursuant  to  [§27]  of  the  Regulation  or  re-deliberation  under  the
Commission’s  authority  pursuant  to  [§27-2]  of  the  Regulation,  the  Commission
shall refund the relevant fine without delay.

2. Reduction of or Exemption from Summary Fine

Where it falls  under any of the following items,  the summary fine may be reduced or
exempted:

(a)  Where  the  unlawful  action  has  occurred  because  of  an  unavoidable  circumstance
such as the system failure;

(b) Where it has been acknowledged that the preventative efforts such as the voluntary
correction or the measure against the relevant officer or employee was made;

(c)  Where  the  member  is  not  responsible  for  the  noncompliance  with  the  reporting
obligation; and

(d) Additionally, where the Chairperson of Commission deems imposition of summary
fines inappropriate.
